## L128 ANE / embedding experiment (2026-09-22)

An **embedding-only int8** variant (`quantize-embedding.py`) keeps Verdict's encoder and decision head FP16. It reduced L128 from **303,210,832 to 264,679,130 bytes**. The four-case native typed smoke passed (worst calibrated error **0.00518**), and a fixed 12-request public Decision Index input sample from seven families agreed with FP16 on **12/12 selections and abstentions** (worst calibrated difference **0.01042**). No gold labels were used. On those requests, full rendering/tokenization/Core ML/calibration p50/p95 was **3.637/3.880 ms FP16** versus **3.627/3.804 ms e8**; this is no meaningful latency improvement on a small exploratory run. The candidate was **not uploaded**. The earlier whole-model LUT8 remains failed at 96/100 selection/abstention agreement.

The embedding candidate's compute plan has **906 of 915** operations eligible on CPU+ANE, but estimated runtime share remains **68.22% ANE / 31.78% CPU**, nearly identical to FP16. Nine CPU fallbacks include int32 preprocessing and one affine-dequantization constant. `coreml-cli` reported 3.77 ms CPU+ANE and 4.05 ms ALL model-call median for e8, versus prior FP16 3.71/3.92 ms; the runs were made under different power conditions and must not be used as a speedup claim. New runs were on battery. Reports: `reports/e8-L128-{conversion,profile,timing}.json`, `reports/verification-verdict_e8_L128_candidates25.json`, and `reports/verdict_e8_L128_candidates25-request-comparison.json`.
